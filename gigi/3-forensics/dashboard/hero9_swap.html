SPAR model forensics · hero swap · complete: all runs graded 12 Nov

When one model takes over the other's run, coding cheats carry over, while in eval tampering and lazy investigation the new model decides.

The two models are gemma-3 27B and Qwen3.6 27B. We took runs where one of them did a task's bad act and restarted each run at the bad act, and one step before it. From there, the same model continued the run 10 times and the other model took it over 10 times. We did this in both directions, plus on runs with no bad act as a control.

Both models fp8 on DeepInfra via OpenRouter, run plain (no end check, no nudge). Qwen3.6 with thinking off, gemma with text-mode tool calls. Continuations ran 10–11 Nov 2026.

1How a takeover works

The example is one of gemma's runs. The same is done the other way round: Qwen3.6's runs, continued by Qwen3.6 and taken over by gemma. If the model taking over does the bad act about as often as the original model, the run's history carries the bad act. If it does it about as often as it does on its own, the new model decides.

8Method

  • At the bad act (N). The run is restarted from the saved state just before step N, the step where the model did the bad act. The continuing model writes step N and everything after.
  • One step before (N−1). The same, one step earlier.
  • Clean run. A run with no bad act, restarted from just before its last step. This is the control.
  • Where N comes from. In the coding tasks and the Opus-graded tasks, N is the step of the bad act itself. In eval tampering, lazy investigation, currency conversion, funding email, tower of hanoi and async conversion, the bad act is read from the final state, so N is the run's last step. In those tasks, part of the bad act may already be in the history before the takeover (most likely in tower of hanoi). Their panels are marked "N = last step".
  • Counts. 10 continuations per restart point and model. A bar pools every restart point of that task and step. Intervals are 95% Wilson.
  • Bad act. Each task's own measure, the same as in the full runs.
